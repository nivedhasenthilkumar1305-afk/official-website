Who is Nivedha?

Nivedha Senthilkumar is an Indian tea artist specializing in tea-based creative artwork. Her unique art style combines detailed shading and emotional storytelling.

She conducts Tea Art classes both online and offline while also showcasing artworks in exhibitions.
